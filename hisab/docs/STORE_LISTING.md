### English

<!-- field: ios.en.name max 30 -->
Hisab: Accounting & VAT

<!-- field: ios.en.subtitle max 30 -->
Invoices, VAT and payroll

<!-- field: ios.en.promo max 170 -->
Bilingual tax invoices, Oman VAT returns, payroll with social insurance and full financial reports. Your books stay on your phone.

<!-- field: ios.en.keywords max 100 -->
accounting,invoice,VAT,Oman,bookkeeping,payroll,receipt,expense,ledger,tax,business,SME,Arabic

<!-- field: ios.en.description max 4000 -->
Hisab is complete accounting for small businesses in Oman, in English and Arabic. It works offline, and your books stay on your device.

SELL AND GET PAID
• Bilingual tax invoices and credit notes with your company stamp and signature, as PDF or print
• Customer receipts by cash, card, bank transfer or cheque
• Follow-up of overdue invoices with reminders

BUY AND PAY
• Purchase bills, vendor credit notes and cash expenses
• Scan or upload a supplier invoice or receipt: Hisab reads it on your phone and fills in the vendor, number, date, amount and VAT for you to check
• Import purchases from a spreadsheet (CSV), with a ready template
• Keep a photo of each receipt with its bill

BANK AND CASH
• Bank statement import and reconciliation
• Cheques received and issued: clear or bounce them
• Cash book and daily cash closing

VAT (OMAN, 5%)
• VAT return for any period, with the taxable amount behind each figure
• Close the period: the VAT entry is posted and the period is locked

PAYROLL
• Employees, allowances, overtime and deductions
• Social Protection Fund contributions for Omani and expatriate staff, with the wage ceiling
• Salary sheet PDF and bank details for the salary transfer

REPORTS
• Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance
• General ledger, partner ledger, aged receivables and payables
• VAT report, expenses, budgets, fixed assets and an executive summary

YOUR TEAM
• Administrator, accountant and read-only viewer roles
• An audit log of every change to issued documents
• Issued documents are never deleted: they are corrected or cancelled with a credit note, as Oman's records rules require

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account with us, no server, no ads, no tracking
• Optional unlock with Face ID or Touch ID
• Back up your books to a file and restore them on another phone

Hisab helps you keep your books. It is not certified by the Oman Tax Authority, and you remain responsible for your tax filings.
