Common questions

I forgot my password

Ask an administrator to open Users & Access, choose your user and set a temporary password. If the only administrator forgot theirs, restore your latest backup or contact us.

How do I move my books to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings › Export Backup. On the new phone: choose Restore Backup on the welcome screen.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

See delete your data.

Does Hisab file my VAT return?

Hisab prepares your VAT return figures box by box. You then submit them on the Oman Tax Authority portal.
